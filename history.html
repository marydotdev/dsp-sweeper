Sweeper History

Add past days

Today is saved automatically while the Cortex routes page is open. For earlier days, choose one of these:

Manually (no automation)

  1. Open the Cortex routes page.
  2. Click Cortex's date box (next to the station name) and pick an earlier day.
  3. Wait for that day's routes to load. The day is saved and shows up here.
  4. Repeat for each day you want, then switch Cortex back to today.

Looking at past days doesn't change your sweeper toolbar.

Automatically automates your browser

The extension takes over your open Cortex routes tab and changes its date to each missing day, one after another (about 10 seconds per day). It saves each day, then returns the tab to today. Don't use that tab while it runs. It uses your Cortex sign-in, so check that automated use fits Amazon's terms for your DSP.
